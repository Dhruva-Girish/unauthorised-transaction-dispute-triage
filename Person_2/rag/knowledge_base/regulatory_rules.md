# Regulatory Compliance & Legal Framework (Regulation E & Banking Mandates)

## 1. Electronic Fund Transfer Act / Regulation E Rights
- **Notice Window**: Cardholder must report unauthorized electronic fund transfers within 60 calendar days of transmittal of the periodic statement showing the unauthorized debit.
- **Liability Caps**:
  - Timely notice within 2 business days of learning of card theft: Cardholder liability capped at $50 / equivalent local currency.
  - Notice after 2 business days but within 60 days of statement: Liability capped at $500.
  - Notice beyond 60 days: Cardholder may bear full liability for unauthorized transfers occurring after the 60-day window.

## 2. Provisional Credit Requirements
- If an investigation cannot be completed within 10 business days of notification, provisional credit must be granted to the customer while the formal investigation continues.

## 3. Grounding & Anti-Fabrication Mandate
- Disputed facts must never be presumed against the cardholder without documentary verification.
- Internal AI systems must distinguish external legal policy standards from verified database records.
- Triage decisions must document the regulatory basis for assigned priorities.
